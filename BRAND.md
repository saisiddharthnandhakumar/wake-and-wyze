## 1. Brand Overview

**Wake & Wyze** is a premium functional coffee brand built for modern professionals who demand more from their daily coffee ritual. The brand combines specialty instant Robusta coffee with Lion's Mane mushroom extract to create a beverage that supports sustained energy, focus, and mental clarity.

**Website:** [wakeandwyze.com](https://wakeandwyze.com)

**Positioning:** The intersection of specialty coffee, functional nutrition, and modern productivity. Wake & Wyze is **not** a medicinal or supplement product — it is a premium everyday ritual.

**Category:** Functional Coffee (new category between premium coffee and functional wellness)

---

## 2. Vision & Mission

### Vision
To become the world's most trusted functional coffee brand by helping millions of people start every day with greater clarity, focus, and intention.

### Mission
To redefine the daily coffee ritual by combining exceptional coffee with thoughtfully selected functional ingredients, creating products that fit seamlessly into modern, high-performance lifestyles.

---

## 3. Brand Philosophy

Most people drink coffee simply to wake up. **Wake & Wyze believes coffee should do more.** Your morning coffee should become the foundation of your most productive hours — not just another caffeine hit.

Instead of relying on multiple cups throughout the day, Wake & Wyze creates a smoother, more intentional coffee experience through the combination of premium coffee and Lion's Mane mushroom extract.

**Minimal, modern, premium.** Scandinavian-inspired with generous whitespace. The brand aesthetic should resemble premium technology and design brands rather than traditional wellness products.

- **Status:** Even semantic colors stay within the warm, earthy palette — no bright reds or greens
- **No cold blues, no harsh black** anywhere in the system

## 12. Gradients & Effects

### Page Body Background
Two faint radial gradients over `surface`:
- Bronze (`rgba(150, 85, 42, 0.05)`) bleeding from top center (80% × 50% ellipse)
- Sage (`rgba(92, 106, 75, 0.03)`) from bottom-right (60% × 40% ellipse)

### Pipo Gradient Background (`pipo.tsx`)
Full-screen layered radial gradients on a `#FAF9EF` base:
- Peach tint: `rgba(230, 176, 147, …)`
- Muted green tint: `rgba(150, 175, 130, …)`
- Cream highlight: `rgba(250, 249, 239, …)`
- Heavily blurred (8cqmin) with two grain-noise SVG passes (blend mode: overlay)
- Creates soft, tactile, paper-like depth

### Hero Shader (`shaders-hero-section.tsx`)
- Animated `MeshGradient` in 7-color warm palette (espressos, copper, sage, camel)
- Wireframe overlay in brand colors
- Static linear-gradient fallback for reduced-motion
- Bottom fade to cream for seamless section transition
- SVG glass-effect filter on eyebrow badge
- Pulsing border ring in `#96552A`, `#F6F1E7`, `#5C6A4B` on `#241811`

### Selection Style
Sage background (`#5C6A4B`) with white text.

### Motion
- Scroll-snap: `y proximity` on viewports ≥ 600px
- Smooth scrolling
- Reduced-motion: all animations/transitions collapsed to `0.01ms`

- **Positioning:** Prefers "functional coffee" over "mushroom coffee"
